About Me

I am Shailesh Kushwaha, a Ph.D. Research Scholar in the Department of Mechanical Engineering at the Indian Institute of Technology Madras (IIT Madras), where I conduct research under the supervision of Prof. C. Balaji.

My academic and research background is in thermal engineering and thermal management of electronic systems. My work combines experimental investigations with computational modeling and data-driven methods to address challenging heat-transfer problems.

During my research career, I have designed and developed in-house experimental facilities for electronics and data center cooling. I have also developed computational models and used experimental measurements for model validation. Building on this foundation, my doctoral research has expanded toward machine-learning-assisted reduced-order modeling for rapid thermal prediction.

I am particularly interested in developing computational approaches that can bridge the gap between high-fidelity physics-based simulations and fast predictive models. Looking ahead, I am interested in scientific machine learning and Physics-Informed Neural Networks (PINNs) for developing efficient alternatives or complements to conventional CFD.

Research Direction

My long-term goal is to develop intelligent computational frameworks for thermal engineering that integrate experimental data, physics-based modeling, reduced-order methods, and scientific machine learning.

I am particularly interested in developing physics-informed approaches that can provide the predictive capabilities of high-fidelity simulations at substantially lower computational cost.
